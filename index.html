Inside the experiment

Volumetric XPBD: The slice is solved using Extended Position Based Dynamics over 1,440 tetrahedra. Hydrostatic tetrahedral constraints enforce strict volume preservation while edge compliances model hyperelastic rebound and rind stiffness.

Optical Absorption & Refraction: A two-pass WGSL pipeline evaluates physical thickness per pixel via back-face depth reprojection. Transmitted light follows the Beer–Lambert law ($I = I_0 e^{-\sigma d}$) with IOR 1.34 Snell refraction and thin-edge subsurface scattering.

Anatomical Embedding: Twelve 3D teardrop seeds and internal micro-bubbles are coupled directly to the deformation gradient of their containing tetrahedra, rotating and stretching naturally with the flesh.

Floor Dynamics: Ground contacts feature unilateral non-penetration penalties, Coulomb friction, and dynamic contact ambient occlusion with warm caustic halos.
